# Caught Up design thesis

## Calm editorial intelligence with a clear sense of completion

Caught Up should feel like a composed European technology briefing that has
already done the filtering. The interface is a finite edition: it opens with a
dated editorial judgment, distinguishes change from consequence, keeps
evidence within reach, and ends with permission to stop.

The identity is built from five behaviors:

1. **Editorial hierarchy before interface chrome.** Serif headlines and a
   measured reading column establish authority; the neutral sans handles
   navigation, metadata, tables, and controls; mono is reserved for dates,
   identifiers, and measured values.
2. **The final period as a product mark.** A blueprint-blue dot represents the
   final item, a resolved status, and the sentence “You’re caught up.” It is
   used consistently, never as a pulsing activity indicator.
3. **Evidence is visible but not noisy.** Sources, corrections, uncertainty,
   and provenance use precise rules and compact labels. They do not compete
   with the opening paragraph.
4. **Density follows the task.** Today and article pages breathe; Radar is
   comparative; Topics are curated; Weekly has cover hierarchy; Archive and
   Search are efficient; Sources and Glossary behave like reference indexes;
   Promotion remains an operator utility.
5. **Completion is an end, not a reward.** The closing state uses a quiet
   typographic pause, the period mark, and a secondary feed action. There is no
   streak, confetti, badge, or urgency loop.

## Composition

- Light-first canvas with warm reading paper and cool structural neutrals.
- Hairline rules create sections; stronger boundaries are reserved for
  corrections, sponsorship, source evidence, and completion.
- A wide publication grid contains a reading measure near 68ch and a compact
  evidence rail only when content supports it.
- Controlled asymmetry comes from masthead/date columns, numbered Briefs,
  signal annotations, and selected media crops—not from overlapping fake UI.
- Low radii support controls and small media. Most editorial sections remain
  unboxed.

## Motion

Motion is brief and orienting: entry opacity/translation, dialog state,
reading progress, and a single completion emphasis. It uses CSS or existing
small listeners, respects reduced motion, and never competes with prose.

## Anti-positioning

Caught Up is not a SaaS landing page, AI chatbot, developer blog, cyberpunk
terminal, live trading surface, social feed, or collection of rounded cards.
It avoids gradients, glass, glow, fake charts, generic AI imagery, decorative
metrics, marketing superlatives, and generated product screenshots.

## Route intent

- **Today:** one complete editorial arc.
- **Article:** durable reading and verification.
- **Radar:** real movement and recurrence, expressed with accessible text,
  lists, bars, and timelines.
- **Topics:** recognizable editorial dossiers rather than raw tag buckets.
- **Weekly:** a distinct cover and summary of the week.
- **Archive/Search:** dense retrieval.
- **About/Sources/Glossary/Corrections/Health:** trust and reference.
- **Promotion:** dense, gated, copy-first operations.
