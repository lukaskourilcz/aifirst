One day, one to three articles, and a Saturday

The „before“ is the live front page of 26. 9. 2026 recreated from source. The „after“ mockups use the real 25. 9. edition (headline, dek, Briefs, Watchlist, photo). The 2- and 3-article days are simulations that borrow the real 13. 9. and 19. 9. editions as the day's second and third articles, because no real day has more than one yet. Section labels follow the assignment rule in DECISIONS.md. Where a slot has no real image (16. 9., a drawn plate), the mockup shows the honest fallback, not a picture.

Rules illustrated: the lead headline always sits on the lead photo, in white Space Grotesk 700 on a flat ink band (rgba(20,22,26,.84); white on it measures ≥ 10:1 even over white pixels), so contrast never depends on the photograph. Secondary headlines are Source Serif 4 600. Mono is reserved for dates, times and credits. Section names replace the numbered rail. Briefs and Watchlist belong to the day, not to an article, so they stay in one place whatever the article count.
