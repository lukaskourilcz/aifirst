Pre-launch design audit · DNESKAi (caughtup-ai.vercel.app · lukaskourilcz/aifirst@main) · 27. 9. 2026

What to cut, what to fix, what to leave alone

Nine sections audited in order, one page each: the current view recreated from the live site and source, then the findings (delete list, copy table with Czech replacements, UX, visual). Three before/after mockups: Home on a weekend, the article masthead + source ledger, and the navigation rail. Followed by the design-system audit and a prioritized action list. Light theme only — the product has no dark theme.

Verdicts

01 · Homepage · /Needs work

Weekday arc is right; on the weekend it announces a failed publication, and the rail carries four modules that are not today's edition. Mockup 1.

02 · Issue · /articles/…Needs work

The editorial apparatus is the right shape; English leaks into the ledger, ids are shown as labels, items repeat, and nothing says the text is machine-written. Mockup 2.

03 · Weekly · /weekly, /tydenNeeds work

/weekly shows an English May digest as „nejnovější“ four months later; /tyden is the working one. Two surfaces, three names.

04 · Radar · /radarNeeds work

A dashboard built from n=1 tag counts, a two-month-old price table and green status dots. Cut to one list or redirect to Topics.

05 · Topics · /topicsNeeds work

Seven calm cards behind an engineering intro; detail pages stack five tag-arithmetic lists; the English tag mapping makes the counts wrong.

06 · Archive + SearchNeeds work · P0

The list is fine. Twelve „systém“ rows print the pipeline's English failure reasons and link to the operator site — the most damaging thing a reader can find.

07 · Lessons · /lekceNeeds work · copy only

A good glossary in a gamified frame: „60 / 60“, terms „odhaleno“, a cycling „Dnes“. Structure and glosses stay.

08 · Trust pages · /about, /sources, /corrections, /health, /glossaryNeeds work

About is well written but silent on who writes; Sources shows wrong citation counts and weight scores; Corrections is right; Health reads as an operator page.

09 · Global chromeNeeds work

A good rail component with a bad inventory (12 items, 4 thin or empty), four dead social icons, three names for About, /cs/ leftovers in OG. Mockup 3.

Design system audit

Tokens are disciplined; the drift is ten kicker classes, six chips, five row components. Six system-level changes.

Prioritized action list

10 × P0 · 20 × P1 · 7 × P2, each pointing back to its finding. Plus what was not verified.

What should stay exactly as it is

The paper canvas, hairline rules and zero radius · the three typefaces and their roles · the editorial highlights block (Proč na tom záleží / Co se změnilo / Co zůstává nejisté) · the numbered DigestRow and SectionMasthead · the lead package and overlay plate · /tyden's day groups and week chain · the /corrections ledger and its empty state · the /about ladder · the rail component, drawer, palette and keyboard overlay · the honesty of the no-edition state, once it stops firing on weekends.

Method

Live routes fetched on 27. 9. 2026 (a Saturday): /, the 25. 9. article, /radar, /sources, /lekce, /akce, /weekly, /topics, /archive. Every other route and state read from the repository (app/, components/, lib/i18n/dictionaries.ts, globals.css, config/, public/data/board/). Recreations use the product's own tokens and fonts; photos are placeholders. Where something could not be seen, the finding says „not verified“. English is the report language; every replacement string is Czech.
