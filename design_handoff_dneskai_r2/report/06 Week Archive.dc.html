Group by day; the day's lead gets the picture

Both pages get a fixed 200 px date column on the left and the articles on the right, so a day with three articles and a day with one read on the same grid. In Poslední týden the day's priority-1 article is a row with a 280 px 3:2 cover and dek; the others are compact rows with 96 px squares. The Archive is denser: title, section and time, a 56 px square; days without an edition stay as one quiet „bez vydání“ line; a section filter row at the top replaces the Témata index page. The 25. 9. day is simulated with three real articles, as on the front page.
